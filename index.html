04 Packaging

Packaging principles

CTR begins with a clear promise, not visual noise.

CTR is a response to a package in context, not a thumbnail score. I review the title and image together against traffic source, topic demand, and mobile legibility.

  1. 01

    Focal point

    Give the eye one immediate subject or idea before adding supporting detail.

  2. 02

    Hierarchy

    Control scale, contrast, and placement so the intended reading order survives at mobile size.

  3. 03

    Density

    Remove information that competes with the central promise instead of treating every detail as equally important.

  4. 04

    Relationship

    Let the title and thumbnail add different parts of the same honest promise rather than repeating one another.

06 Process

How I work

A five-step review built for editorial decisions.

The goal is not a long report. It is a well-supported recommendation that can be understood before publishing and evaluated afterward.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Review performance and establish channel, format, and traffic context.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Determine whether friction sits in packaging, content, distribution, or audience fit.

  3. 03

    Test

    Define the smallest meaningful variable that can answer the question.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Compare the response in context rather than relying on an isolated metric.

  5. 05

    Recommend

    Translate the finding into a direct next action for the editor or channel owner.
